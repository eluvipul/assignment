## Step 1: Identify the given probabilities

- Probability of buying headphones: $P(H)=0.30$
- Probability of buying a smartphone given that the user bought headphones: $P(S\mid H)=0.40$

## Step 2: Apply the conditional probability formula

$$
P(S\mid H)=\frac{P(S\cap H)}{P(H)}
$$

Since the question already states that 40% of headphone buyers later buy a smartphone, this is directly the conditional probability we need.

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{P(S\mid H)=0.40=40\%}
$$

Interpretation: There is a 40% probability that a randomly selected user will buy a smartphone, given that they have already bought headphones.

def bayes_churn_probability(prior_churn, prob_email_given_churn, prob_email):
    if prob_email <= 0:
        raise ValueError("prob_email must be greater than 0")

    # Bayes' Theorem
    posterior = (prior_churn * prob_email_given_churn) / prob_email

    return posterior


# Test the function
result = bayes_churn_probability(
    prior_churn=0.15,
    prob_email_given_churn=0.6,
    prob_email=0.25
)

print(f"Probability of churn given email: {result:.2%}")

## Step 1: Identify the given probabilities

Let:

- $F$ = Transaction is fraudulent
- $A$ = Alert system flags the transaction

Given:

- Probability of fraud: $P(F)=0.02$
- Probability of an alert when fraud occurs: $P(A\mid F)=0.90$
- Probability of a false alert: $P(A\mid \neg F)=0.05$
- Probability of a normal transaction: $P(\neg F)=1-0.02=0.98$

## Step 2: Apply Bayes’ Theorem

$$
P(F\mid A)=\frac{P(A\mid F)P(F)}{P(A)}
$$

First, calculate the total probability of an alert:

$$
P(A)=P(A\mid F)P(F)+P(A\mid\neg F)P(\neg F)
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
P(A)=(0.90\times0.02)+(0.05\times0.98)
$$

$$
P(A)=0.018+0.049=0.067
$$

## Step 3: Calculate the probability of fraud given an alert

$$
P(F\mid A)=\frac{0.90\times0.02}{0.067}
$$

$$
=\frac{0.018}{0.067}\approx0.2687
$$

## Final answer

$$
\boxed{P(F\mid A)\approx26.87\%}
$$
