## 2. Calculate the sample mean

In Excel or Google Sheets, enter:

excel

```
=AVERAGE(A2:A6)
```

Calculation:

$$
\text{Sample Mean}=\frac{250+480+150+720+350}{5}
$$

$$
=\frac{1950}{5}=\boxed{₹390}
$$

## 3. Compare sample mean with population mean

| Measure | Formula | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample mean (5 orders) | `=AVERAGE(A2:A6)` | ₹390.00 |
| Population mean (20 orders) | `=AVERAGE(A2:A21)` | ₹509.50 |
| Difference | ₹509.50 − ₹390.00 | ₹119.50 |

## 4. Interpretation

The sample mean is ₹390, while the population mean is ₹509.50. The sample mean is ₹119.50 lower because the first five orders happen to have smaller amounts on average than the full dataset.

Conclusion: A sample mean can differ from the population mean depending on which observations are selected. A larger, representative sample generally provides a more reliable estimate of the population mean.

4.
Population vs. Sample: In our Zomato order dataset, the population consists of all 20 order amounts recorded for the day, while the sample consists of the first 5 order amounts selected from those 20 orders. A population includes every observation of interest, whereas a sample is only a subset of the population. For a food delivery company like Zomato, using a sample is often more practical because the company handles thousands or millions of orders daily, making it faster and less expensive to analyze a representative subset of orders to understand customer spending patterns.
