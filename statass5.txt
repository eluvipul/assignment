5.
Conditional probability means the probability of an event happening when we already know that a particular condition is true.

In this Flipkart example:

Without the condition: The probability that a user adds a phone to their cart is 20%.

Given that they are interested in mobiles: The probability increases to 50%.

This means that users known to be interested in mobiles are more likely to add a phone to their cart than users in general. We use the information about their interest to update the probability.
