Question 1. a correlation matrix in Excel, let’s consider the following example. You are the stock
analyst in the investment bank. Your manager recently asked you to analyze the correlations
between prices of stocks that can be potentially added to the portfolio
You then analyze the stocks of the following companies: Zorto ,Maxp ,Survelia and Jecco . 

Interpret the Results
High correlation (close to 1.0) → Stocks move together (e.g., Maxp & Jecco at 0.70).

Low correlation (close to 0) → Stocks move independently (e.g., Survelia & Jecco at 0.25).

Negative correlation (<0) → Stocks move in opposite directions.

For portfolio diversification, you want low or negative correlations to reduce risk.
