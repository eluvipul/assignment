Interpret results
High correlation (close to 1) → stocks move together (less diversification).

Low correlation (close to 0) → stocks move independently (better diversification).

Negative correlation (<0) → stocks move in opposite directions (useful for hedging).

Example:

Zorto & Maxp (0.82) → strongly related.

Jecco & Zorto (0.40) → more independent, good for diversification.
